## Failure Taxonomy Implications
- Keep `inline proof`, `external verifier`, `replay/state check`, and `judge grading` as separate failure layers.
- Keep `completion signal` and `benchmark pass` explicitly decoupled in attribution.
- Treat checkpoint/resume substrate as a separate axis from correctness proof.
- Keep BigAI claims labeled `behavioral reconstruction`.
