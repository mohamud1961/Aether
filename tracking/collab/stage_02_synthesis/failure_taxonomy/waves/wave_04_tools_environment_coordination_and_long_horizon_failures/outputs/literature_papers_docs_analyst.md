- terminology_and_definition_notes:
  - pre-action authorization: deterministic policy decision before a tool/action executes, distinct from post-hoc evaluation and distinct from sandbox containment.
  - containment boundary: technical execution limits (filesystem/network/process scope) independent of approval policy.
  - orchestration-level verification: verification applied to multi-agent collective output and plan coverage, not only single-call correctness.
  - delegation contract: role-specific scope on context, capabilities, and authority at handoff boundaries.
  - long-horizon degradation: cumulative context/cost/coordination drift over many steps where local decisions can cause global failure.
- benchmark_definition_notes:
  - DeepPlanning evaluates long-horizon planning with hard global constraints where a single critical miss can invalidate the plan.
  - VMAO defines Plan-Execute-Verify-Replan with explicit stop conditions and timeout/configuration caps.
  - Utility-guided orchestration frames action selection and stopping as explicit quality-cost control policies.
  - OAP evaluation frames per-action authorization under adversarial pressure rather than relying on alignment or sandboxing alone.
