# Stage 03: Evals And Variants

Purpose: convert synthesis findings into measurable work.

This stage was used for:

- custom eval contracts;
- known-bad and baseline checks;
- verifier/grader design;
- variant family seeds;
- tournament boards;
- scorecards and keep/kill decisions;
- sentinel selection.

Exit condition: a variant cannot be implemented or promoted until the target
eval surface and regression sentinels are explicit.

Subfolders:

- [skills](skills/) - eval and variant governance skills;
- [prompts](prompts/) - eval-oriented specialist prompts;
- [artifacts](artifacts/) - score surfaces and decision records.
