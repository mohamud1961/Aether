# Deep Synthesis: Trajectory Case Studies

Use this skill when runs need to be turned into explicit case studies that
support mechanism or failure synthesis.

## Trigger

Use when the task is to connect runs to source or, if source is unavailable,
to careful behavioral reconstruction.

## Inputs

Bring:

- run paths or result rows;
- source links where visible;
- per-run notes or comparison notes;
- the target mechanism or failure family;
- confidence caveats about missing source or incomplete logs.

## Workflow

1. Keep the case study concrete.
2. Compare runs against each other before generalizing.
3. Record the first decisive divergence when relevant.
4. Separate source linkage from behavioral reconstruction.
5. Tie the case study back to mechanism or failure implications.

## Outputs

A useful case study should include:

- case id or family name;
- systems or runs compared;
- outcome profile;
- per-run notes;
- cross-run comparison;
- failure-point comparison;
- source or architecture links;
- behavioral reconstruction caveats;
- mechanism or failure implications.

## Validation Checklist

- the case study is not just a generic summary;
- source linkage is explicit when available;
- missing source is labeled as a caveat, not hidden;
- cross-run comparison adds real signal;
- the case study is specific enough to support later synthesis.

## What Not To Do

- do not claim source-backed certainty when source is missing;
- do not publish raw private trajectories;
- do not treat one run as a universal case;
- do not smuggle final taxonomy claims into the case-study layer.
