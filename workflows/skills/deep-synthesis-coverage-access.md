# Deep Synthesis: Coverage Access

Use this skill when the corpus needs routing, access repair, or coverage
discipline before synthesis can be trusted.

This is the public-safe version of the coverage-access support track.

## Trigger

Use when you need to answer:

- what evidence classes are reachable;
- which families are still thin or unread;
- what the next routing work is;
- whether a support gap is blocking a wave or just needs carry-forward.

## Inputs

Bring:

- the current coverage register or coverage state note;
- corpus inventory summaries;
- route maps for source, trajectory, informal, and eval evidence;
- any prior synthesis or prep notes;
- the list of unread or weakly covered families.

## Workflow

1. Separate access work from analysis work.
2. Map the corpus into broad evidence classes and route families.
3. Mark what is complete, partial, or still blocking.
4. Keep unread families visible instead of silently assuming closure.
5. Update the coverage status after materially changing access or routing.

## Outputs

A good coverage-access pass should leave behind:

- a current routing decision;
- explicit support gaps;
- prioritized next source families;
- reusable route-map notes;
- a clear statement of what is still only support work.

## Validation Checklist

- support work did not become final analysis;
- route maps stay explicit;
- unread or weakly covered families remain visible;
- support completion is not mistaken for artifact completion;
- the next wave can cite coverage status honestly.

## What Not To Do

- do not make final mechanism or failure claims;
- do not collapse support work into the main synthesis claim set;
- do not hide partial access behind a confident summary;
- do not treat the register as more complete than it is.
