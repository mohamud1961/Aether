# Deep Synthesis Skill Family

Use this family when a research or synthesis phase needs to move from
inventory to evidence-backed claims.

It is intentionally split into several small skills instead of one generic
"deep synthesis" note. The family mirrors the phase artifacts used in the
private synthesis work while staying public-safe.

## Family Members

- [Coverage access](deep-synthesis-coverage-access.md) - route the corpus and
  keep access work separate from analysis
- [Evidence inventory](deep-synthesis-evidence-inventory.md) - bind the corpus
  onto evidence dimensions and confidence labels
- [Mechanism map](deep-synthesis-mechanism-map.md) - extract structured
  mechanism families and interactions
- [Failure taxonomy](deep-synthesis-failure-taxonomy.md) - turn runs and
  evidence into failure families and attribution rules
- [Source-system dossiers](deep-synthesis-source-system-dossiers.md) -
  summarize the source-visible systems that shape the synthesis
- [Trajectory case studies](deep-synthesis-trajectory-case-studies.md) -
  connect runs to source or careful behavioral reconstruction
- [Adjudication and audit](deep-synthesis-adjudication.md) - run the checklist
  and preserve contradictions before accepting a wave
- [Wave closure and decision package](deep-synthesis-wave-closure.md) - hand
  off accepted claims, warnings, and next-step obligations

## Family Rule

Do not use this family as a generic summary prompt.

Use [deep-synthesis-loop](deep-synthesis-loop.md) when an orchestrator needs to
run several of these lanes together, assign specialist threads, perform
contradiction review, and close with accepted claims plus follow-up actions.

Each member should have:

- a trigger;
- inputs;
- a bounded workflow;
- concrete outputs;
- a validation or review checklist;
- explicit "what not to do" guardrails.
