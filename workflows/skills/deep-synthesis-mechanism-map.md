# Deep Synthesis: Mechanism Map

Use this skill when evidence is ready to be turned into structured mechanism
families and interactions.

## Trigger

Use when the task is to explain how a system works across runs, source
material, and supporting notes, not merely to summarize outcomes.

## Inputs

Bring:

- the evidence inventory;
- trajectory analyses;
- source reconstruction notes;
- literature or formal theme notes when relevant;
- informal or issue-driven contradiction pressure;
- any prior mechanism draft or carry-forward warnings.

## Workflow

1. Identify candidate mechanism families.
2. Separate source-backed mechanisms from behavioral reconstruction.
3. Preserve interactions between families instead of flattening them.
4. Saturate the family only when the evidence is broad enough.
5. Keep contradictions and regime-local limits visible.

## Outputs

A useful mechanism-map pass should include:

- mechanism families and subfamilies;
- interaction notes across families;
- confidence and contradiction status;
- open questions or boundary cases;
- carry-forward warnings for downstream artifacts.

## Validation Checklist

- mechanisms are operationally described, not vaguely named;
- simple and complex mechanisms are both visible;
- interactions are explicit;
- the artifact preserves source-versus-behavioral caveats;
- promoted families are clearly labeled exploratory, emerging, or decision-ready.

## What Not To Do

- do not collapse mechanisms into anecdotes;
- do not hide source-visibility differences;
- do not mark a family decision-ready because it appears in many summaries;
- do not erase contradictions to make the map read cleanly.
