# Deep Synthesis: Wave Closure And Decision Package

Use this skill when an accepted wave needs a structured handoff to the next
artifact or phase.

## Trigger

Use after adjudication says the wave is ready to close, or when a follow-on
artifact needs a compact decision package.

## Inputs

Bring:

- the principal synthesis;
- the carried-forward claims and contradictions;
- the evidence gaps to keep visible;
- the coverage frontier;
- the next artifact target.

## Workflow

1. Record what the wave resolved.
2. Record what remains open.
3. Preserve warnings and contradictions.
4. Package the handoff so the next artifact can inherit safely.
5. State whether a follow-up decision or another wave is required.

## Outputs

The decision package should include:

- accepted claims;
- preserved contradictions;
- unresolved questions;
- evidence gaps;
- carry-forward warnings;
- next artifact or next action;
- any required handoff receipt.

## Validation Checklist

- the next artifact can inherit without reopening the whole wave;
- warnings are visible instead of hidden in prose;
- open questions remain explicit;
- the handoff does not overclaim closure.

## What Not To Do

- do not treat closure as the same thing as universal completion;
- do not drop contradictions to make the package look clean;
- do not hide unresolved evidence gaps;
- do not skip the handoff receipt.
