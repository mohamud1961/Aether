# Deep Synthesis: Adjudication

Use this skill when a synthesis artifact needs a checklist-based review before
it is accepted.

## Trigger

Use after a wave or artifact has enough material to review, but before the
claim set is treated as stable.

## Inputs

Bring:

- the wave or artifact outputs;
- the principal synthesis;
- contradiction notes;
- evidence inventory and coverage frontier notes;
- any audit checklist or handoff schema used for the phase.

## Workflow

1. Check packet discipline and scope honesty.
2. Check coverage honesty.
3. Check evidence-and-claim separation.
4. Check whether the wave actually answered its question.
5. Decide whether the next artifact may open.

## Outputs

A useful adjudication pass should leave:

- a pass / pass-with-warnings / blocked verdict;
- visible contradictions or warnings;
- explicit carry-forward obligations;
- a clear next-step recommendation.

## Validation Checklist

- major claims are traced to concrete evidence;
- observations and inference stay distinct;
- contradictions remain visible;
- confidence matches evidence quality;
- the artifact improves the cumulative state instead of just adding prose.

## What Not To Do

- do not mark a wave pass because the file is long;
- do not invent certainty to fill a checklist;
- do not hide coverage gaps;
- do not collapse review and production into one step.
