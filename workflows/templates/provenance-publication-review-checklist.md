# Provenance Publication Review Checklist

Use this when adapting source material or preparing a public-safe release note.

## Inputs

- source tree or source surface read;
- target files changed;
- validation evidence;
- claims or wording under review.

## Checklist

- Record exact source files read.
- Record exact target files changed.
- State what was ported, adapted, or deliberately excluded.
- Confirm which claims were qualified or withheld.
- Verify license or notice obligations have been handled or explicitly deferred.
- Sweep public text for private paths, raw ledger references, hidden graders,
  and eval-sensitive wording.
- Confirm the public prose does not imply affiliation or parity with the
  source project.

## Closeout Output

- provenance summary;
- withheld material list;
- validation notes;
- open publication gaps;
- next slice.
