interesting mental model from the goat of mental models for learning/intelligence

lots of interesting threads on agentic learning:
- build-verify loops are an autonomous optimization process to solve any task. Specify a goal & verification mechanism via tests and let the loop run to iteratively write code that improves itself

- Just-In-Time everything via overfitting.  The loop is a general purpose discovery mechanism for solutions to be generated on the fly.  Build an agent that’s tuned perfectly to every narrow task.  There’s levels to this from in-context stuffing, to updating the harness, to finetuning

- Generalization as adaptation.  Related to above, generalization sort of becomes local adaptation/overfitting per task.

It’s cool to see the first sparks of “goal to code” coming to life today, the latest models are just smart enough and just autonomous enough in a good harness to allow this “agentic machine learning” loop
