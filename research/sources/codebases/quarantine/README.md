# Quarantined Codebase Mirrors

This directory holds research mirrors that may still be useful for mechanism or workflow analysis, but should not be treated as normal trusted reference codebases.

## Inclusion rule

A codebase may be placed here when it has one or more of:

- provenance risk
- legal or licensing uncertainty
- unusually low maturity
- unclear trustworthiness as engineering evidence
- strong workflow or mechanism interest despite those risks
