# Global Quality Gate Checklist

Every accepted record must pass all gates below.

1. JSON is valid and matches schema.
2. Source is inside the primary window or has a specific foundational exception reason.
3. Source has auditable provenance and a stable canonical URL or canonical locator.
4. Source is primary or near-primary, not commentary about commentary.
5. Source contains at least one concrete mechanism or concrete failure signal.
6. Claims are source-local, atomic, and traceable to explicit locations.
7. Claims are labeled `measured`, `asserted`, or `anecdotal`.
8. `reason_included` and `relevance_note` are factual and not synthetic mini-reviews.
9. Bucket fit is real; cross-bucket signal is tagged in metadata, not used to justify weak fit.
10. `benchmark_contamination_risk` is assigned and plausible.
11. Duplicate underlying sources are not accepted twice under different URLs or wrappers.
12. Sparse buckets are allowed to stay sparse; padding with weak sources is a failure.
