03 / WHAT THIS SUPPORTS

One narrow claim, and a useful next test.

This case supports that GPT-5.6 Luna + Aether has a preserved official pass on the named task, and that Aether's historical review machinery could still mishandle a task-visible success.

The verified Terra record makes the motivating observation more precise, but it does not establish that Luna is generally better than Terra, that Aether is generally better than Codex, or that the difference was caused by the harness. The three-month programme is designed to test those questions under matched conditions.
