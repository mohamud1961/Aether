01 / THE SYSTEM

Let the intelligence lead.
Design everything else around it.

Aether separates three jobs that current agents often blur together.

Let the model lead.

001
INTELLIGENCE

It chooses the approach, changes course and decides what it needs next.

Give it the computer.

002
CAPABILITY

Tools, memory, recovery and room to extend its working environment.

Keep action bounded.

003
BOUNDARY

Sandboxed execution, explicit permissions, isolation and complete traces.

THE RESEARCH THESIS

More capable agency should not require more hidden intelligence around the model — or less control around its actions.

Aether is testing whether a model can lead more of its own work while the system stays bounded, inspectable and reliable.
