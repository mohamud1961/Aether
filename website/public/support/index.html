01 / WHAT SUPPORT ENABLES

Dedicated time for a fair test.

Funding supports paid research time, frontier-model access, controlled evaluation infrastructure and the work of turning raw runs into inspectable evidence. Cash support matters because a three-month programme needs sustained attention; compute credits can complement it but do not replace it.

The budget will be costed before any application is sent. It will name one requested amount, the currency, the period covered and the scope that amount makes possible. No award or payment has been received.

THE OFFER

Research freedom, visible work.

Support is sought on a non-equity basis, with experimental choices kept independent and any continuation beyond the first three months agreed separately.

02 / THREE-MONTH OUTPUTS

What the programme will leave behind.

The outputs are research artifacts, not a promise of a positive result. A failed hypothesis is a useful result if the method makes the failure legible.

BASELINE

A frozen protocol.

Task fixtures, model settings, environments, budgets, validity rules and independent grading.

COMPARISON

Scored result rows.

Repeated matched trials with capability, review, recovery, latency, spend and invalidity kept distinct.

PUBLICATION

Methods and limits.

A concise report with retained mechanisms, rejected ideas, failures and shareable evidence.
