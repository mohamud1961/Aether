# Variant Decision Rubric

This page gives a public-safe way to read curated variant summaries.

## Keep

Keep a variant when:

- it improves the target rows;
- it does not damage the sentinel;
- it stays clean on contamination;
- its step or cost impact is acceptable for the family.

## Keep For Follow-Up

Keep a variant for follow-up when:

- it improves the target rows, but not enough to promote yet;
- or it has a promising shape but still needs a cleaner sentinel pass.

## Kill

Kill a variant when:

- it fails to move the target rows at all;
- or it regresses a sentinel without a compensating gain;
- or it violates contamination or validity expectations.

## Promote

Promote a variant only when:

- target movement is real;
- the sentinel board stays clean;
- the scoreboard is contamination-safe;
- the public evidence is strong enough to justify carry-forward.
